General Portal Inquiries

How do I register for campus hackathons and workshops?

Navigate to the Events page from the navigation bar. Click on any event card to view rules, schedule, and team requirements. Hit the RSVP Now button to confirm your registration using your student ID.

Can I update my registered email and contact number?

Yes. Go to your Profile page and click Edit Profile. For official student ID and enrollment changes, please contact the FTE Academic Registrar office.

What are the minimum browser requirements for StudentHub?

StudentHub is built with modern web standards (HTML5, CSS Grid, ES6+). It supports Google Chrome 90+, Mozilla Firefox 88+, Microsoft Edge 90+, and Safari 14+ across desktop and mobile devices.

Who do I contact if I get locked out of my account?

Use the Contact Us form under the Technical Support category, or reach out to the campus IT department in Building C, Room 102.
